# Public aggregate outputs

This folder contains aggregated, non-sensitive outputs for public release and reproducibility.

- `data_state_level.csv`: state-level central-scenario electricity demand and attributable CO2 emissions.
- `data_BA_level.csv`: balancing-authority-level central-scenario electricity demand and attributable CO2 emissions.

Both files use:

- the central facility-load scenario, `u = 0.58`;
- EPA eGRID2023 Revision 2;
- the location-based **total-output CO2** attribution basis;
- approximately 81.8 TWh and 25.7 Mt CO2 in aggregate;
- an implied HDC-weighted carbon intensity of approximately 314 gCO2/kWh.

The files contain aggregate regional summaries only. They do not contain facility identifiers, addresses, coordinates, or facility-level records.
